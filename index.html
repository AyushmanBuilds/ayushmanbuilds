About the founder

Meet Ayushman Acharya

I'm Ayushman Acharya, the founder of Ayushmanbuilds, a web development and digital growth brand focused on building practical digital experiences for businesses. I combine hands-on development with SEO, performance, conversion-focused design and business automation.

I personally work across the full project lifecycle — understanding the business, planning the experience, writing the front-end and back-end code, integrating services, optimizing the site for search engines, and deploying the finished product. The goal is not to deliver another template, but to create a website or web application that has a clear purpose and can grow with the business.

Through Ayushmanbuilds, I work on custom websites, e-commerce platforms, web applications, SEO optimization, website revamps and automation workflows for businesses across India. I also build integrations using tools such as Firebase, Razorpay and EmailJS when a project needs more than a static website.

My approach is straightforward: understand what the business needs, build cleanly, keep the experience fast and mobile-friendly, make the important information easy for customers and search engines to understand, and provide support after launch.
